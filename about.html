About Repair Partner

We started Repair Partner to fix a simple problem: getting something repaired in Pune shouldn't mean three phone calls, a lost afternoon, and a technician who never quite shows up.

Built around one idea: repairs shouldn't be your problem to manage

Most repair experiences put the work on you — finding a technician, negotiating a price, following up on delays, and hoping the fix actually holds. We built Repair Partner to remove every one of those steps.

Today, our technicians handle everything from kitchen appliances to power tools, picking items up from your doorstep and returning them tested, repaired, and backed by a written warranty — no showroom visit required.
